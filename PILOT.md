# TraceSworn paid pilot

Goal: sell and deliver three paid individual matters, measure delivery hours and repeat demand, then offer a defined retainer to repeat buyers. There is no established evidence of demand or profit yet.

## Offer and qualification

Start with US litigation/insolvency counsel handling a current matter involving supplied public wallet addresses or exchange clues. Ask for a matter reference, counsel's authority, valuation date and expected output. Decline recovery guarantees, requests to move funds, unsupported tracing promises and matters outside actual professional competence. Use a written scope before work starts.

Discovery is $2,500 for one person, one date, up to 10 supplied public addresses and one revision. EVM historical values, undisclosed accounts, tokens and cross-chain activity may need separately sourced evidence. Evidence Package is $6,500 under an agreed scope. Premier is $10,000/month for the capacity documented on /for-firms; no unlimited labor.

## Low-workload routine

- Once: configure services, apply schema, verify domain/email authentication, confirm billing, load and qualify pilot prospects.
- Each workday: read the single operations digest, check the monitored reply inbox, review ready report drafts, handle exceptions, and log actual specialist time. Time required depends on case volume and complexity; no hours-per-week promise is supported yet.
- Each week: examine paid conversions, total delivery hours, actual external costs and outstanding obligations. Pause new work when included capacity is exhausted.
- Each month: complete screening outcomes, reconcile actual subscription receipts and expenses, review recurring demand and re-scope unprofitable engagements.

## Validation targets, not forecasts

Three paid firms, repeat purchases, and total delivery costs below 50% of collected revenue. At a $125/hour loaded internal cost, 25 Premier specialist hours cost $3,125 before client management, data, sales, insurance, tax and overhead. Do not treat a modeled labor margin as accounting profit. If demand or delivery economics fail the pilot, adjust scope/price before scaling acquisition.

## Launch acceptance checks

Apply the whole schema, test concurrent monthly submissions at the 4/50 limits, test the 25-hour gate, submit findings for paid and unpaid cases, verify draft job recovery, ensure new findings hide prior approval, approve the latest report, verify the sealed token evidence, and test unsubscribe and uncertain-send suppression with owned test mailboxes. Confirm daily digest delivery. Database and email acceptance must be run with real configured services before client onboarding.
